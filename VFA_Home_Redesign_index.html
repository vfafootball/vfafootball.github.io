About VFA

Our Mission

VFA Football exists to build a competitive Football Fusion league where players and franchises can enjoy organized competition, meaningful statistics, strong media coverage and a clear set of rules.

Our mission is to keep improving the league while making sure everyone has a fair, competitive and fun experience within VFA. That means creating clear standards, recognizing player and team achievements, protecting league integrity, improving communication and giving the community a place where competition and enjoyment can exist together.

VFA is built around continuous improvement: better administration, better game reporting, better statistics, better media and a better experience for the people who make the league possible.

Official League Rules

VFA Rulebook

CFFL is the baseline rule framework. VFA-specific rules below override the baseline where stated.

1. Introduction & League Authority

VFA Football is a Football Fusion league. These rules establish the league's competition, conduct, scheduling, account-integrity and disciplinary standards. The CFFL framework is used as the baseline unless a VFA-specific rule below changes it.

Priority rule: When a VFA rule conflicts with the CFFL baseline, the VFA rule controls.

2. Server Rules

  • No spam or disruptive flooding.
  • No hate speech, graphic/gory content, NSFW content or doxxing.
  • No admin abuse.
  • No misuse of tickets or staff systems.
  • No trolling that interferes with league operations.
  • No unauthorized advertising.

3. VFA Game Rules

  • Formats: VFA supports 7v7 and 8v8.
  • Regulation: four quarters of five minutes each, for 20 minutes of regulation.
  • Clock: standard NFL clock principles apply except for VFA's four-by-five-minute format.
  • Downs: standard four-down system.
  • First down: 15 yards are required for a new set of downs.
  • Fourth down: if the offense has seven yards or fewer remaining to the line to gain, it may attempt the conversion rather than being required to punt.
  • Failed fourth down: possession changes at the spot where the play ends.
  • Possession: interceptions and fumbles follow standard NFL possession principles.
  • Scoring: touchdown = 6; after a touchdown the team chooses a +1 kick or +2 conversion; field goal before a touchdown on the possession = 3; safety = 2. Other scoring follows standard NFL principles unless modified here.
4th & 25: the CFFL-style procedure is used. The team must use /mm 4th to announce the attempt. A 4th-down call made without the required announcement is challengeable and subject to the applicable penalty.
  • Voting, 1% fumble, no-boundary, Noon, weather and Classic settings follow the league baseline where applicable.

4. Scheduling & Forced Times

  • Scheduling issues should be handled through a ticket/thread and staff should be pinged when required.
  • Forced game times are based on approved league scheduling windows.
  • Teams should be notified at least 30 minutes before a forced game time.
  • The CFFL baseline windows are 7:00–10:00 on weekdays and 7:00–10:30 on weekends, unless VFA staff publishes a different official window.
  • Server switching may be requested before kickoff or at halftime. Ping/lag evidence should be documented, including screenshots where required.

5. Challenges & /mm

  • Only the Franchise Owner or authorized coaching staff may issue a challenge.
  • Challenges must be made visibly using the required /mm procedure.
  • A challenge that is not properly announced is invalid.
  • False or failed challenges can result in the applicable point penalty.
  • Useless or spammed /mm challenges can be penalized.
  • Post-game challenges require the evidence and timing required by league procedure.

6. Accounts, Verification & Exploits

  • Alternate accounts and account-sharing intended to bypass league rules are prohibited.
  • Creating a new persona or using another Discord/Roblox account to evade a restriction is treated as an account-integrity violation.
  • Cheating, exploiting or using prohibited game exploits is forbidden.
  • Players should complete league verification before participating when required.
  • Verification records are used for account integrity and anti-raid protection.

7. Gameplay Rules

  • Walk-ons are prohibited unless VFA staff explicitly authorizes an exception.
  • Lag warnings may lead to a player being benched for a quarter or the remainder of a game depending on severity.
  • Lag sacks are illegal and challengeable.
  • Milking is restricted to the approved late-game situation: up by 14 with 45 seconds or less in the fourth quarter, with the play clock handled according to the challenge procedure.
  • Trucking and hit-sticking are legal where the game settings permit them.
  • Play action is legal.
  • Routing out of bounds to confuse defenders is illegal; a scoring play involving an illegal routing action can receive the applicable point penalty.
  • Double blocking follows the CFFL-style formation limits, including the inside-hash/line-of-scrimmage restrictions.
  • Offensive-line and tight-end alignment rules follow the league baseline, including the applicable 10- and 15-yard formation penalties.

8. Statistics & Games Played

  • VFA statistics are intended to be generated from official game reports.
  • The baseline games-played/stat-cap framework is retained unless VFA staff publishes a Series-specific exception.
  • The CFFL baseline treats games beyond the approved statistical limit as stat padding and disregards the excess for standard statistics.
  • Playoffs and conference championships may be treated separately under league policy.
  • Future VFA JSON score reports will update standings, player statistics, point differential, ratings and MatchMVP records automatically.

9. VFA Overtime

  • Teams alternate possessions.
  • If a team scores a touchdown, the game ends immediately.
  • If a team scores a field goal, the other team receives the next possession.
  • Alternating possessions continue until a touchdown ends the game.
  • If the required possession sequence remains tied, VFA uses a mandatory five-kick tiebreaker.
  • Each team receives five alternating kicks. The team with more makes wins.
  • If still tied after five kicks, alternating kicks continue until a winner is determined.

10. Mercy, Discipline & Penalties

  • 35-point mercy rule: the standard mercy threshold is 35 points. If the trailing team elects to continue, the game may continue under league rules.
  • 50-point stat-padding rule: a point difference above 50 is treated as stat padding under the baseline, with the applicable statistics reduction.
  • Forfeits, illegal walk-ons, alting, hacking/cheating, admin abuse, doxxing and possession of cheats carry the corresponding disciplinary consequences under the VFA penalty schedule.
  • Disbanding/leaving a franchise with an FO can trigger the applicable suspension/bail process, subject to VFA staff review and any published exceptions.
Exact VFA monetary/Robux bail amounts and suspension lengths should be finalized in the league's published penalty schedule before enforcement. The CFFL document provides baseline examples, but VFA has not separately confirmed that every amount is identical.
