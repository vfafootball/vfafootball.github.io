HOW TO PLAY

Controls & positions

QB: WASD to move and scramble; mouse aim + click or Space to throw.
WRs: Run routes automatically; lead them with your throws.
Offensive line: Five linemen form a pocket and slow down pass rushers. Move out of the pocket when it collapses.
Single-player: The CPU controls all defenders.
Local multiplayer: Player 1 controls the offense; Player 2 moves the selected defender with arrow keys and switches defenders with Shift.
Scoring: Reach the end zone, finish four quarters, then progress through the season. Progress saves in this browser.
