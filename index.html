The Start of a Dynasty

Baltimore • A new era of dominance

Baltimore is making a serious statement, with Surmise leading one of the league’s most impressive quarterback campaigns. He has recorded 36 touchdowns (2nd), just 3 interceptions (1st), 2,517 passing yards (2nd), and a 66% completion rate (1st)—production and efficiency that have made him a favorite in the MVP race. His playoff performance adds another major chapter to the story: his original 12 postseason touchdowns are doubled by the league’s 2× playoff stat multiplier, giving him 24 playoff-adjusted touchdowns. The offense has another standout in Envy, who leads the league with 13 receiving touchdowns (1st) and 655 receiving yards (1st), alongside 31 receptions (8th). With Surmise controlling the offense and Envy turning opportunities into game-changing plays, Baltimore’s rise feels bigger than one hot run. The foundation of a dynasty may already be taking shape.
